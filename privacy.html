Videos2Social

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Videos2Social ("the app") is a self-hosted tool that runs entirely on the computer of the person using it. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to remove it.

Data the app accesses

When you connect an account, the app receives only what is needed to publish your videos:

  • YouTube (Google): an OAuth access token and refresh token, plus the ID, name and profile picture of the YouTube channel you choose. Scopes requested: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.force-ssl (set thumbnails and captions, change a video's publish time, read your channel name).
  • Facebook (Meta), when connected: a Page access token plus the ID, name and picture of the Pages you choose, used to publish videos to those Pages.
  • TikTok, when connected: an access token and refresh token, your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and the creator settings TikTok returns before posting (nickname, allowed privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch settings, maximum video length). Scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.publish (post a video you choose) and video.upload (send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft).

The app does not access your contacts, email, comments, analytics, payment details, or any videos it did not upload.

How the data is used

Tokens are used only to carry out actions you start in the app: uploading a video you created, setting its title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions, scheduling or changing its publish time, and showing which channel or Page it was posted to. The data is never sold or shared, and never used for advertising, profiling or training models.

Storage and security

  • All data is stored locally on your own computer, in a database inside the app's folder. The app has no server of its own and sends no data to its developer or to any third party.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and OAuth client secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. On macOS the encryption key is kept in the system Keychain.
  • The app's web interface only accepts connections from the same computer (127.0.0.1).
  • Video files, titles and descriptions go only to the platform you choose (YouTube, Facebook or TikTok), over HTTPS, through their official APIs. On TikTok, every post's caption, privacy level and interaction settings are chosen by you before posting.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account. Clicking Disconnect on the app's Accounts page revokes the token with Google and deletes it, together with the app's local posting history for that account.
  • You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections (or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), for Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations, and for TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Deleting the app's folder deletes all of its data. Videos already published stay on YouTube, Facebook or TikTok until you remove them there.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

This website and the app use no tracking cookies or analytics.
