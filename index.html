Videos2Social

Render once, publish everywhere.

A personal, self-hosted studio for animated explainer videos.

Videos2Social is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It turns written scripts into short animated videos, then lets the owner upload and schedule those videos to their own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Renders videos, thumbnails and captions locally.
  • Connects to the owner's YouTube channel with Google OAuth, their Facebook Pages with Facebook Login, and their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads a video with its title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions, and sets the time YouTube or Facebook should publish it. For TikTok, which has no scheduling, the app uploads the video at the chosen time with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings the owner picked.
  • Shows the upload and publishing status of each scheduled video.

Who can use it

Videos2Social is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it has no server that stores user data. The only people who use it are its owner and the people who manage the owner's channels, each on their own computer.

How it uses Google and YouTube data

Videos2Social uses YouTube API Services only to upload videos the user created, set their thumbnails and captions, set or change their publish time, and read the name and picture of the connected channel. See the Privacy Policy for details.
